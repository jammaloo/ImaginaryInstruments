Play invisible instruments
with your own body

Your webcam tracks your face and hands. No video ever leaves your device — everything runs locally in your browser.

Air Trombone

The trombone hangs off your mouth. Reach out a hand to grab the slide — pull it in and out to change pitch, and open your mouth to blow.

🪗

Air Accordion

The accordion stretches between both hands. Pull your hands apart and squeeze them together to pump the bellows and play.

🪇

Air Maracas

A maraca in each hand. Shake them — sharp back-and-forth shakes rattle the beads, and harder shaking plays louder.

🥁

Air Drums

A kit across the bottom of the frame. Punch down into a pad to hit it — and nod your head for the kick drum.

🎶

Air Harp

A harp hangs in the air below your face. Sweep your fingertips across the glowing strings to pluck them — all ten fingers welcome.

🎸

Air Bass

A bass hangs across your chest. Slide your hand along the neck to choose the note, then flick down over the strings to pluck.
