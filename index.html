Play invisible instruments
with your own body

Your webcam tracks your face and hands. No video ever leaves your device — everything runs locally in your browser.

Air Trombone

The trombone hangs off your mouth. Reach out a hand to grab the slide — pull it in and out to change pitch, and open your mouth to blow.

🪗

Air Accordion

The accordion stretches between both hands. Pull your hands apart and squeeze them together to pump the bellows and play.

First load downloads the tracking models (~15 MB), then everything is instant.
